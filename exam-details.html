📖 About the Exam

JEE Main is a national-level entrance examination used for admission to undergraduate engineering and other programs at participating institutions.

✅ Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidates should have passed or be appearing in the required qualifying examination.
  • Physics and Mathematics are important subjects for B.E./B.Tech.
  • Candidates must satisfy the admission requirements of the respective institute.
  • Always verify the current year's official information bulletin.
